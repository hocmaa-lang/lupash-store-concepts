How it plays
Two to five players, 30 to 50 minutes. Move along the trail, meet what the trail throws at you, and answer the card you draw. Children who like winning have something to win; children who need a reason to talk have a reason.
Good for
Children who find direct questions about feelings uncomfortable.
Families who want something to do together that is not a screen.
Therapists, counsellors and school staff working one to one or in small groups.
In the box
100 question and event cards
1 game board
1 die
WHY IT WORKS AT THE TABLE

The questions are the game

They give children a reason to talk about confidence, worry, frustration and pride without being asked to sit down and discuss their feelings. Playing gives them somewhere else to look while they answer.
