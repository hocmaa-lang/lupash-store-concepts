How it plays
Two to six players, 20 to 30 minutes. Children collect sets and answer a question about whichever feeling they are holding, which is a much easier ask than naming a feeling from nothing. If you can play Go Fish you can play this, so children can teach each other and adults can stay out of it.
Good for
  • A first step for families who have not tried anything like this before
  • Travelling, waiting rooms and anywhere a board will not fit
  • Therapists wanting a short, contained activity
